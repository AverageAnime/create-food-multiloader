### ***2.5.0***

_Note: These interactions are also available through recipes._

* Hold an item in your main hand and a compatible item in your offhand, then `RMB` to craft. The crafted result is placed in your main hand.
    * Crafting recipes with two ingredients are supported by default.
      * Enable `enable_single_handcraft` to also allow single-ingredient.
    * A particle effect plays on craft. Can be toggled.
    * Use the config to control the items affected.
