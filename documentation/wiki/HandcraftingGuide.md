### ***2.5.0***

---

* Hold an item in your main hand and a compatible item in your offhand, then `RMB` to craft. The crafted result is placed in your main hand.
    * Crafting recipes with two ingredients are supported by default.
    * Enable `allow_single_item` to also allow single-ingredient recipes.
* A particle effect plays on craft, and can be turned off.

---

Which recipes can be handcrafted is controlled by a filter and an exclude list — see [Config Options](Config-Options).
