### ***2.7.0***

---

Disabled by default. Enable in `createfood-server.toml`.

* Hold a raw ingredient in your main hand, and use `Shift` while standing near a block tagged `farmersdelight:heat_sources` to cook it.
    * Detection range is 3 blocks horizontally and 1 block vertically from your feet by default.
    * Holding `Shift` can be turned off.
    * Progress persists as long as you stay within range and keep the item in hand. Switching items or stepping away resets progress.
    * Smoke particles rise while cooking; flames appear and a crackle sound plays on completion.

---

Ranges, the `Shift` requirement, and which recipes can be cooked this way are all configurable — see [Config Options](Config-Options).
