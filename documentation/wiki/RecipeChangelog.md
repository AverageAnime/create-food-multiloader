### ***3.0.0***

---

## Recipe Chains and Ingredients

* Milling `beetroot` now yields red dye, beetroot seeds and shredded beetroot.
* Removed `cocoa_beans` → `chocolate_chips` cutting recipe.
* Removed `condensed_milk` alt recipes that used `milk_powder`.
* Removed all mixing recipes that creates a filled bucket, bottle, or bowl.
* `*_jam` now uses heat.
* `*_juice` now uses 1 fruit down from 4.
* `*_waffle` cooking recipes updated to match mixing recipes.
* `cacao_butter` and `cream_cheese` now yields 4 from a bucket instead of 3.
* `cheese_block` now uses vinegar.
* `cheese_potato_dumplings` and `egg_dumplings` cooking recipes now yield 2.
* `chocolate`, `dark_chocolate`, and `white_chocolate` now uses sugar.
  * `chocolate` and `white_chocolate` now use milk powder.
* `cocoa_powder` cutting now yields 3.
* `cream_cheese` now uses heavy cream.
* `dark_chocolate` now uses cacao butter.
* `fried_pita_bread`, `donut_base` and `chocolate_donut_base` now use a `vegetable_oil_bottle`.
* `graham_cracker` and `chocolate_graham_cracker` cooking recipes now yields 8.
* `ground_beef` now cuts from `beef_cuts` rather than `minced_beef`.
* `heavy_cream` no longer requires heat and produces skim milk.
* `ice_cream_stick` now yields 2 per bowl instead of 3.
* `kelp_soup` now uses 250mb water instead of 1000mb.
* `milk_powder` is now made with skim milk and heated compacting.
* `paprika` now comes from a pepper slices rather than whole peppers.
* `pasta`, `macaroni`, `marshmallow`, `magma_cream_marshmallow`, `gelatin` and `boiled_egg` cooking recipes now use a water bottle.
* `raw_*_pizza` and `raw_*_calzone` now use raw toppings.
* `raw_cinnamon_sweet_roll_base` now uses butter.
* `shakshuka` now uses 1 egg down from 2.
* `yogurt` now uses 4:1 milk to vinegar consistently.

## Cross-Mod Recipe Compatibility

Create, Cultural Creators, Cultural Delights, Delightful Creators and Farmer's Delight recipes are now changed to match this mod.

* All recipes now use tags and carry load conditions.
* Fix `hot_cocoa` and `cake` recipes using Fabric fluid amounts and other fluid duplication.
* Fruits Delight and My Nether's Delight popsicles now compact in a Create basin.
* Fruits Delight, Hearth & Harvest and Veggies Delight juices now press in Expanded Delight's juicer and can be mixed by hand.
* Recipes calling for an apple, carrot, potato, beetroot, tomato, onion, mushroom or fungus now also take this mod's sliced, diced and shredded forms.
* Recipes calling for sugar now take `c:sugar`.
* Removed recipes that skip a step this mod's chains require.
  * `cookie`, `pie_crust` and `wheat_dough` no longer come from raw wheat.
  * `hamburger`, `sweet_berry_cheesecake`, `chocolate_pie` and `pasta_with_mutton_chop` now go through their assembly.
* Rice dishes now take `c:cooked_rice`.
* `apple_pie` no longer uses flour, and Farmer's Delight's no longer takes three raw wheat on top of its pie crust.
* `beef_patty` and `minced_beef` now come from compacting `beef_meatball` and `raw_beef_meatball`.
* `blueberry_muffin` and `cranberry_muffin` now bake from `muffin_batter`.
* `bread` now bakes from any wheat dough.
* `cookie`, `honey_cookie` and `sweet_berry_cookie` compacting recipes now require heat and use `c:sweet_dough`.
* `frying_oil` now comes from `vegetable_oil`.
* `glow_berry_custard` now requires heat and uses `c:egg_yolk`.
* `hot_cocoa`, `apple_cider` and `melon_juice` now bottle from a bucket.
* `pie_crust` now bakes from `raw_pie_crust`.
