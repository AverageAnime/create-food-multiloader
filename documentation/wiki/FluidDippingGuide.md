### ***2.7.0***

* Use `RMB` on a small bowl with a filled container to pour it in. Holds maximum 4000mb.
    * Any container with a `create:emptying` recipe works, and that recipe sets the amount.
* Fluid cannot be added if it does not fit in full, or if the bowl already holds a different fluid.
* Use `RMB` with a food item to dip it, based on `create:filling` recipes. One item is converted per use, and the result goes to your inventory.
* Use `RMB` with an empty container to take fluid back out.
* Breaking a bowl holding exactly 1000mb leaves a fluid source block behind. Anything else, the fluid is lost.
